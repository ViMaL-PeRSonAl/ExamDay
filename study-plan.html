Phase 2: University Theory Series Plan

Minimum 3 Full Days Dedicated Per Theory Subject

5 University Papers
SLOT A • EXAM: 23 NOV (MON, 1:30 PM)

Computer Network (PCCST501) • 3 Days Dedicated

• Day 1 (Nov 10–11 preview + Nov 21): Physical & Data Link layer, Framing, Error detection (CRC, Hamming code), Multiple access (CSMA/CD, CSMA/CA).
• Day 2 (Nov 22): Network layer, IP addressing (IPv4/IPv6, CIDR Subnetting problems), Routing algorithms (Dijkstra, Distance Vector, BGP).
• Day 3 (Nov 23 morning + Nov 22 night): Transport layer (TCP Reno, Flow control, Congestion window), Application layer (DNS, HTTP/2, SMTP).

SLOT B • EXAM: 27 NOV (FRI, 2:00 PM)

Design & Analysis of Algorithm (PCCST502) • 3 Full Days Dedicated

• Day 1 (Nov 24): Asymptotic notations (Big O, Omega, Theta), Master Theorem, Divide & Conquer (Merge sort, Quick sort, Strassen's matrix), Greedy (Kruskal, Prim, Huffman).
• Day 2 (Nov 25): Dynamic Programming (0/1 Knapsack, Longest Common Subsequence, Matrix Chain Multiplication, Bellman-Ford).
• Day 3 (Nov 26): Backtracking (N-Queens, Graph Coloring, Hamiltonian), Branch & Bound (Traveling Salesperson), and NP-Completeness (P vs NP, Cook's theorem).

SLOT C • EXAM: 01 DEC (TUE, 1:30 PM)

Deep Learning Concepts (PCCMT503) • 3 Days Dedicated

• Day 1 (Nov 28): Linear algebra for deep learning, Perceptron, Activation functions, Backpropagation mathematics & gradient descent variants (Momentum, RMSProp, Adam).
• Day 2 (Nov 29): Convolutional Neural Networks (LeNet, AlexNet, VGGNet, ResNet residual skip connections), Batch Normalization.
• Day 3 (Nov 30): Recurrent Neural Networks, LSTM gating mechanisms, GRU, Autoencoders, and Attention mechanism overview.

SLOT D • EXAM: 03 DEC (THU, 1:30 PM)

Introduction to Computer Vision (PBCMS04) • 3 Days Distributed Strategy

• Day 1 (Pre-studied Nov 29 evening): Image Formation, Geometric camera models, Perspective projection, Image Filtering (Gaussian, Laplacian, Sobel, Canny edge detection).
• Day 2 (Pre-studied Nov 30 evening): Feature extraction & Matching (Harris Corner, SIFT, HOG, RANSAC model fitting).
• Day 3 (Full dedicated day on Dec 02): Object detection (Sliding window, R-CNN, Fast/Faster R-CNN, YOLO), Image segmentation (Otsu, Watershed, U-Net) & Final formula recap.

SLOT E • EXAM: 07 DEC (MON, 1:30 PM)

Software Project Management (PECST521) • 3 Full Days Dedicated

• Day 1 (Dec 04): Project evaluation (Cost-benefit, NPV, ROI), Process models (Waterfall, Spiral, Agile/Scrum sprints, Kanban), Software effort estimation (COCOMO I & II, Function Point Analysis).
• Day 2 (Dec 05): Activity planning (Work Breakdown Structure, Network planning, Critical Path Method - CPM, PERT calculations, Float/Slack, Resource allocation & Gantt charts).
